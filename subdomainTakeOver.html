Security Finding

Subdomain Takeover Demonstrated

This page is being served from a subdomain belonging to Arnergy as a proof of concept for a subdomain takeover vulnerability identified.

Finding: The DNS CNAME record for was pointing to a Vercel deployment that no longer existed. This allowed an external party to claim and serve arbitrary content under this trusted domain.

An attacker could have used this to serve phishing pages, harvest credentials, hijack session cookies scoped to the parent domain, or conduct social engineering under a trusted brand URL.

Reported by: Digiss LLC Security Team  |  Classification: High Severity  |  Status: Proof of Concept Only
